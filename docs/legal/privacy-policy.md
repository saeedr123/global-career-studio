## What we collect, and why

- Account details you supply, such as your name and email address,
  to run your account and let you sign in.
- CV and letter content you supply, such as your profile, documents,
  job versions, and letters, to build, save, and export the documents
  you ask for.
- Photos you upload, if you choose to include a photo in a document,
  to include that photo in your document and export only.
- Usage counts, such as how many documents you created and which
  template or country pack you used, to run the service and to
  understand which features are used. Analytics never read CV
  content. Analytics are based on counts and choices only, never on
  the text of your CV, letter, or job description.
- Support messages you send, to answer your request.

We do not collect payment details in this version, because Stripe
payments are on hold, no payments are taken yet, and no checkout,
payment form, or Stripe integration is working. Prices are not set.

## AI use and training

The selected AI provider is Muse AI. Connected Muse AI is ready for
a key and is NOT live until the owner adds a key and sets a budget
cap. Until then, the writer runs locally in your browser, and no
user CV text is sent anywhere.

When connected AI is enabled, it will be used only to help rephrase
text you already supplied, under the same guardrails as the local
writer: it must never invent qualifications, employers, job titles,
dates, certifications, or achievements, missing fields are prompts,
and a suggestion is applied only after your confirmation. Letters
and writer output must be checked by you before use.

No training on user CVs happens without separate explicit consent.
Your CV text is not used to train any model unless you give that
separate explicit consent first, in addition to agreeing to this
policy. If you do not give that consent, your CV text is not used
for training.

## ATS check

The ATS check is a heuristic check only. It is not a real ATS, not
a score, and not a pass or fail result, and it gives no guaranteed
ATS outcome. A pasted job description used for that check is used
for that check only, and is not stored as part of your CV.

## Admin access

Admin access to user documents is logged, for support with a
recorded reason only. Admin logs contain the action, the time, and
the reason. Admin logs contain no CV content and no secrets. No
admin access is used for any purpose other than support with a
recorded reason.

## Account deletion

You may ask for your account to be deleted. Account deletion
includes your photos, your exports, and your AI logs, as well as
your profile, documents, job versions, and letters. The exact
backup expiry statement is OWNER TO CONFIRM, see
docs/legal/owner-to-confirm.md, and it must be published alongside
this policy before launch.

## Downgrade and your own documents

Downgrading keeps viewing and downloading of documents you already
created. You are never locked out of viewing your own CV. Downgrade
locks premium editing, premium templates, and premium exports only.
