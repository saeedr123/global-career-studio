## Trial

- Trial starting default: 7 days.
- Card: no card is required to start the trial.
- Limit: one trial per account.
- What the trial unlocks: all 10 version 1 templates, full letter
  types, advanced AI in config, and PDF and DOCX export, as set in
  the billing config. Advanced AI in this disclosure means connected
  Muse AI once it is live; until a key and a budget cap are set,
  only the local writer runs, and no CV text is sent anywhere.
- Trial end: when the trial ends, the account moves to Free, unless
  the owner has enabled a paid plan and the user has chosen one.
  Downgrade keeps viewing and downloading of documents already
  created, and you are never locked out of viewing your own CV.
  Downgrade locks premium editing, premium templates, and premium
  exports only.
- Trial cancel: cancelling a trial keeps trial access until the
  trial end date, then moves the account to Free, and never charges
  or contacts a payment provider, while payments are on hold.
