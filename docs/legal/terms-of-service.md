## The service

Global Career Studio is a tool for building CVs, resumes, cover
letters, and related career documents from text you supply, using
templates, country packs, language packs, a local writer, a letter
studio, and a heuristic ATS check. Its first launch is on
nvconsult.eu. A separate domain may be added later.

The same free tier applies to everyone, in both editions, with no
second public price list. NVConsult adds human help, as described
in docs/human-review-scope.md. No special Studio benefit for
NVConsult clients is offered at launch beyond that human help.

## Your content and your responsibility

You supply your own career history, and you remain responsible for
it. The tool must never invent qualifications, employers, job
titles, dates, certifications, or achievements, and missing fields
are prompts, not filled values. You must check every document,
letter, and writer suggestion before you use it, send it, or rely
on it, including checking names, dates, employers, and claims
against your own records. Letters that must be checked and signed
by the sender, such as reference and recommendation letters, must
not be used until the sender has checked and signed them.

Sample data in the tool is fictional and marked SAMPLE. It is not
a real user, and it must not be presented as your history.

## What the service does not promise

- No guaranteed job outcome. Using the tool, a template, a country
  pack, or a human review does not promise a job, an interview,
  a visa, or any other outcome.
- No guaranteed ATS outcome. The ATS check is a heuristic check
  only, not a real ATS, not a score, and not a pass or fail result.
- No legal or visa advice. Country packs are formatting guidance
  only, not legal advice, and they state no legal requirement.
  Human review does not cover legal or visa advice, see
  docs/human-review-scope.md.
- No verified translation. Language packs translate interface
  labels and section headings only, are labelled not professionally
  verified, and never auto translate your CV content.

You must keep your sign in details private, and you must not use

## Admin access and logs

Admin access to user documents is logged, for support with a
recorded reason only, and admin logs contain no CV content and
no secrets. Analytics are based on counts and choices only, and
analytics never read CV content. No training on user CVs happens
without separate explicit consent.
