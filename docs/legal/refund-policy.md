## How to ask for a refund, once paid plans are enabled

Once paid plans are enabled, a refund request is made by writing
to info@nvconsult.eu, stating the account email and the plan
bought. The request will be answered with the outcome and, if a
refund is given, with how it is returned. No refund method, fee,
or timing is stated here, because no payment provider, price, or
currency has been confirmed yet, and those details must be added
by the owner and confirmed in the legal check before this policy
goes live.

## Downgrade is not a refund

Downgrading to Free keeps viewing and downloading of documents
you already created, and you are never locked out of viewing your
own CV. Downgrade locks premium editing, premium templates, and
premium exports only. Downgrade on its own does not create a refund.
A refund, once paid plans are enabled, follows the refund window
above, once the owner confirms it.

## Trial

The trial starting default is 7 days, no card, one trial per
account. A trial that needs no card takes no payment, so there is
nothing to refund for the trial itself. If a paid plan starts after
a trial, the refund window above, once confirmed, applies to the
paid plan, not to the trial period.

## Human review payments

Human review turnaround and human review price are OWNER TO

docs/human-review-scope.md. Any paid human review, once its price
is confirmed and once paid plans are enabled, will state in its
own invitation whether it is covered by this refund policy or by
separate terms given with that invitation. No human review refund
term is promised in this document.

## Changes

This policy may be changed, with the changed version published
with its date. A change does not remove a refund right that already
